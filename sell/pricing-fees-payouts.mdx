---
title: "Pricing, fees & payouts"
description: "How operator pricing works on AnyAPI: you set the customer price in USD, the platform takes a commission per sale, and earnings pay out to your PayPal account, from $20."
---

Everything on the seller side is in USD. Every earnings row itemizes your share and the
platform fee.

## You set the price

When you register an endpoint you set the **customer list price in USD per 1,000
requests** (minimum $0.01/1k). Routing is cheapest-first among healthy endpoints on the
same API, up to each endpoint's capacity: price lower to win more requests, higher to earn
more per request. See [how requests reach your endpoint](/sell/operator-endpoint-contract#how-requests-reach-your-endpoint).

### Changing your price

From the endpoint's page in the [dashboard](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell/endpoints)
(or `POST /v1/operator/endpoints/{id}/price`):

- **Decreases apply immediately.**
- **Increases apply after a 24-hour delay.**
- At most **2 price changes per endpoint per rolling 24 hours**.

## The commission split

AnyAPI takes a per-sale commission at your profile's rate (`commissionBps`, default 2000
basis points = 20%). Read your actual rate from your
[operator profile](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell) or `GET /v1/operator/profile`.

Worked example at a $5.00/1k list price and the default 20% commission:

| | Per 1,000 requests |
| --- | --- |
| Customer pays | $5.00 |
| Platform fee (20%) | $1.00 |
| **You earn** | **$4.00** |

## When earnings become withdrawable

- **Earnings accrue on confirmed sales:** every request your endpoint wins and serves
  successfully, including free-tier-funded and agent-paid traffic.
- Accrued earnings show as **Pending** in
  [Earnings](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell/earnings).
- **Months finalize 7 days after they end.** Finalized earnings move to your **Available**
  balance, which is withdrawable.

## Payouts

AnyAPI pays sellers through PayPal only. Connect a verified PayPal account in your own name
from [Sell APIs > Earnings](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell/earnings), and verify your
identity, before you can withdraw. If PayPal isn't available to you, email
[support@getanyapi.com](mailto:support@getanyapi.com) and we'll look at alternatives. See
[Become an operator](/sell/become-an-operator).

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Method | PayPal, paid in USD |
| Minimum withdrawal | $20 |
| First payout | Unlocks 30 days after you become a seller |
| Review | AnyAPI reviews every payout before it's sent |
| Fee | PayPal's fee, taken out of the payout |

Request a withdrawal from the dashboard or `POST /v1/operator/payouts` with
`{"amountUsd": 20}`. Your available balance drops by the full amount, and you receive that
amount minus PayPal's fee. The withdraw dialog shows the fee and what you'll receive before
you confirm. If you don't withdraw, AnyAPI starts a payout of your available balance
automatically once it reaches $20, at most once a month. A month in which you already have a
payout, including a withdrawal you requested or a declined payout, gets no automatic one.
Automatic payouts wait for review like any other.

PayPal's fee for sending a payout, per
[PayPal's fee page](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/paypal-business-fees):

| Where your PayPal account is | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| United States | $0.25 per payout |
| Anywhere else | 2% of the amount sent, at most $20 |
| Unknown (PayPal didn't share your country) | The higher of the two |

Payouts are sent in USD. PayPal may charge you to convert them to your currency.

Every payout, including an automatic one, shows as **Waiting for review** in your payout
history until AnyAPI approves it, then **Sending**, then **Paid**. A declined payout shows
**Declined** with the reason, and a failed one shows **Failed**. In both cases the amount
returns to your available balance.

## Refunds, chargebacks, and adjustments

When money a buyer spent on your endpoint is given back, what you earned from it comes out
of your future payouts.

- **Refunds.** When AnyAPI refunds a buyer's top-up, you give back your share of the part
  of it the buyer had already spent on your endpoint. Any bonus AnyAPI added to the
  top-up is never taken from you.
- **Chargebacks.** A chargeback deducts your share when it opens. If AnyAPI wins the
  dispute, the deduction is restored.
- **Compensation for a bad result.** When AnyAPI compensates a buyer for a bad result
  from your endpoint, the amount comes out of your earnings, capped at what you earned on
  that request.
- **90-day limit.** A refund or chargeback of a top-up paid more than 90 days earlier
  takes nothing from you.

Every deduction and restoration is listed under **Adjustments** in
[Earnings](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell/earnings). Deductions can take your
available balance below zero; while it is negative, no payout is made until later
earnings bring it back above zero.

## Quality affects earnings

If your error rate spikes or quality drops, the endpoint is paused automatically (*Paused
(quality)* in the dashboard) and stops earning until automatic re-tests restore it. A
non-2xx, invalid output, or timeout earns nothing: the request fails over to another
provider and the customer is not charged for your attempt.
