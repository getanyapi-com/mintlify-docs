**Operators** run their own scraper or data service behind an API in the AnyAPI catalog.
When your endpoint serves a request, you are paid 80% of what the customer paid, in USD.
The [Seller Terms](https://getanyapi.com/seller-terms) set out the full rules.

<Info>
  AI agents can run an operator account end to end from
  [`getanyapi.com/operator-onboarding/SKILL.md`](https://getanyapi.com/operator-onboarding/SKILL.md).
</Info>

## How selling works

You plug into an existing catalog API rather than list a new one. Each API has a fixed
request and response format (its *schema*). The ones you can register yourself are listed in
[APIs open to sellers](/sell/apis/index); to sell any other one, email
[support@getanyapi.com](mailto:support@getanyapi.com).

You host an HTTPS endpoint that speaks the API's format and set your own price. AnyAPI
sends each request to the cheapest source first, yours or its own, while your endpoint keeps
finishing requests as reliably.

Customers always see AnyAPI as the provider. Your operator id (`op_...`) is used for
routing, earnings, and payouts, and is never shown to buyers.

## The journey

<Warning>
  Before you go live, every seller passes an identity check (a government photo ID and a
  selfie, with our verification partner Didit) and connects PayPal to get paid. If PayPal
  isn't available to you, email [support@getanyapi.com](mailto:support@getanyapi.com) and
  we'll look at alternatives.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start selling">
    In the dashboard, open [Sell APIs](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell) and click
    **Start selling**, or call `POST /v1/operator/profile`. First tick **I agree to the
    Seller Terms**: you accept the [Seller Terms](https://getanyapi.com/seller-terms)
    before you can register an endpoint or run the registration tests (a person reads and
    accepts them in the dashboard). Registering an endpoint, the registration tests and the
    live trial need no approval.

    AnyAPI approves a seller for paid traffic only once the seller profile has a name, a
    photo, and a description, or the seller has chosen to be listed anonymously. Set the name
    and description with `PATCH /v1/operator/profile` and a body of
    `{"displayName": "...", "description": "..."}`. Upload the photo as the raw request body of
    `POST /v1/operator/profile/photo`: a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image of at most 1 MB.

    To be listed anonymously, tick **List me anonymously** in the seller profile, or send
    `"anonymous": true` in the same `PATCH`. The public sellers pages then show only the animal
    name AnyAPI assigns you, never your name, photo, or description. You still pass the
    identity check and are paid the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your endpoint">
    Pick one of the [APIs open to sellers](/sell/apis/index) and deploy an HTTPS endpoint
    that implements its format. The [endpoint contract](/sell/operator-endpoint-contract)
    covers signed requests, the response envelope, and timeouts. The register dialog in
    the dashboard shows the exact schema for the API you pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register it">
    In [Sell APIs > Endpoints](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell/endpoints), enter the
    API it serves, your base URL (HTTPS, port 443, public host), an optional path (default
    `/run`), and your price in USD per 1,000 requests. Through
    `POST /v1/operator/endpoints` you can also send `capacity`, how many requests your
    endpoint takes at once (default 4). You get a **signing secret, shown
    exactly once**. Store it; your endpoint uses it to verify that requests come from
    AnyAPI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass the registration tests">
    Run the registration tests from the endpoint's page, or with
    `POST /v1/operator/endpoints/{id}/suite-runs`, then read the run at
    `GET /v1/operator/endpoints/{id}/suite-runs/{runId}` until it ends. AnyAPI sends your
    endpoint the API's test inputs, signed: a record it finds, an empty list, each not-found
    reason, each input variant the schema allows, and, for an API that pages, the next page through your own cursor.
    Each answer is checked against the API's schema and, where AnyAPI holds a recorded answer
    for that input, field by field against it. Every answer must arrive within the API's time
    limit and under 8 MB. A failed test says why and which fields differed; fix your endpoint
    and run the tests again as often as you like. A passing run starts the live trial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass the live trial (free)">
    Up to 500 real customer calls are mirrored to your endpoint: you receive the customer's
    request input, never who the customer is. Your answers are compared automatically with
    the current provider's. Customers never see your output during the trial and nothing is
    billed. Your trial passes at **at least 95% pass rate over at least 200 comparisons
    spanning at least 7 days, with zero flags for made-up data or tampered URLs**. A passed
    endpoint then waits, with no traffic, until you go live.

    Read every judged call with `GET /v1/operator/endpoints/{id}/trial`. Each call shows its
    outcome (pass, fail, or inconclusive, which counts neither way), each compared field with
    your value and AnyAPI's, how your endpoint failed the call if it did, and the request's
    `X-AnyAPI-Nonce` header so you can find it in your own logs. The customer's input is never
    shown.

    After a fix, restart the trial yourself with
    `POST /v1/operator/endpoints/{id}/trial/restart`; there is no need to email support. You
    can restart during the trial, or in draft after one; a trial that already passed can't be
    restarted. Earlier calls stay readable but stop counting, and the endpoint goes back to
    draft: it re-enters the trial once it passes the registration tests again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Before you go live: verify your identity">
    Once one of your endpoints passes its trial, complete an identity check from the
    **Getting started** checklist in [Sell APIs](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell): a
    government photo ID and a selfie with our verification partner Didit, plus a sanctions
    screening. You get 3 attempts; after that, email
    [support@getanyapi.com](mailto:support@getanyapi.com). AnyAPI keeps only the result,
    your legal name, the country that issued your ID, and the sanctions result. We don't
    store your ID images or selfie.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Before you go live: connect PayPal">
    In [Sell APIs > Earnings](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell/earnings), click
    **Connect PayPal** and sign in with Log in with PayPal. PayPal is how you get paid. The
    account must be verified, in your own name, and in a
    [country PayPal Payouts supports](https://docs.paypal.ai/growth/payouts/reference/countries-supported-features).
    If its name differs from the name on your ID, we show a warning and check it during
    review; it doesn't stop you. If PayPal isn't available to you, email
    [support@getanyapi.com](mailto:support@getanyapi.com) and we'll look at alternatives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get approved and go live">
    A person at AnyAPI reviews your trial results, your identity check, and your PayPal
    account, then approves your account. Your endpoint then serves real traffic in a
    monitored phase, and fully live while quality holds. You earn from the first request
    you win. If quality drops, the endpoint is paused automatically and re-tested until it
    recovers. You can also pause it yourself with the kill switch on its page. Withdraw
    your earnings to PayPal from $20; the first payout unlocks 30 days after you become a
    seller, and AnyAPI reviews every payout before it's sent. See
    [Pricing, fees & payouts](/sell/pricing-fees-payouts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When AnyAPI's answer looks wrong

If you believe AnyAPI's recorded answer for a registration test or a trial call is wrong,
don't copy it. File a data report with
`POST /v1/operator/endpoints/{id}/data-reports`: send `suiteRunId` and `caseId` for a test,
or the call's `trialComparisonId` for a trial call, plus a `message` saying what is wrong
(and optionally the `field`). A person at AnyAPI reviews it; filing one doesn't change your
results by itself. `GET` on the same path lists your reports and their status.

## Let your agent manage it: seller keys

Under **Connect your agent** in [Sell APIs](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell), create a
seller key and copy the prompt for your own AI agent. With that key your agent can do what the
seller dashboard does: register endpoints, run the registration tests, read trial results and
restart a trial, file data reports, set price and capacity, pause an endpoint, rotate its
signing secret, edit the seller profile, and read earnings, payouts, the identity check status
and whether PayPal is connected.

A seller key can't run APIs as a customer or spend your balance. Four steps stay with a
person in the dashboard: accepting the Seller Terms, starting the identity check, linking
PayPal, and requesting a payout. Your agent never accepts the Seller Terms: while they aren't
accepted, it gives you the link to
[Sell APIs](https://getanyapi.com/dashboard/sell), where you read and accept them, and a seller
key that tries to accept them is refused with that same link. The full seller API, with every
route, its errors, and what a seller key may call, is in
[`getanyapi.com/operator-onboarding/SKILL.md`](https://getanyapi.com/operator-onboarding/SKILL.md).

## What the statuses mean

| Status in the dashboard | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Registered but not serving traffic. Run the registration tests when your endpoint is ready. |
| Tests running | AnyAPI is verifying your responses match the API's schema. |
| Live trial | Real traffic is mirrored to you for free and compared with the current provider. |
| Trial passed | Passed the live trial; receives no traffic until you have verified your identity, connected PayPal, and AnyAPI has approved your account. |
| Live (monitored) | Serving and earning, with quality watched extra closely. |
| Live | Serving and earning on real customer traffic. |
| Paused (quality) | Paused by the automatic quality checks; re-tested and restored automatically when it passes. |
| Retired | Permanently removed from routing. |
