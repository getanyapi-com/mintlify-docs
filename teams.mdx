---
title: "Teams"
description: "Share one balance and one set of API keys with your colleagues, with a role for each person."
---

A Team is a second kind of AnyAPI account. It has one balance, its own API keys, and many
people, each with a role. Keys belong to the Team, so they keep working when the person who
created them leaves.

Your personal account doesn't change. Every person keeps their own personal account, and
can belong to any number of Teams as well.

Use a Team when several people spend from the same budget, or when keys must outlive the
person who made them. Keep working on your personal account when you work alone.

## Create a Team

Open the account switcher at the bottom of the dashboard sidebar and choose **Create Team**.
Enter a name. You become the Team's Admin, and the dashboard switches to the new Team.

A new Team is an empty account:

- Its balance starts at $0. Add funds to the Team before you run APIs from it.
- It has no API keys yet. Create Team keys after you switch to it.
- Your personal balance stays on your personal account. Balances can't be moved between
  accounts.
- Your existing keys and connected apps keep billing your personal account. To share work,
  fund the Team and use Team keys.

A personal account can't be turned into a Team.

## Switch accounts

The account switcher at the bottom of the sidebar lists your personal account and every Team
you belong to. Choose one to act for it. Everything in the dashboard then shows that account:
its balance, usage, request history, keys, and billing.

API keys and connected apps don't follow the switcher. A key always spends the account it
was created in. See [Keys belong to an account](/api-keys#keys-belong-to-an-account).

## Invite people

Admins invite people from the Team's **Members** page:

1. Switch to the Team and open **Members**.
2. Choose **Invite**, then enter the person's email address and pick a role.
3. AnyAPI emails them an invitation.

Pending invitations are listed on the **Invitations** tab. An Admin can resend or revoke one
there. The role comes from the invitation, so the person joins with the role you picked.

### Accept an invitation

Open the link in the invitation email. Sign in, or sign up if you don't have an AnyAPI
account yet, and the dashboard switches to the Team.

If you're already signed in, pending invitations also appear in the account switcher. Choose
**Accept** to join.

## Roles

Every member has one role. Everyone on a Team sees the same account data; roles limit who can
spend and who can change things.

| Action | Admin | Developer | Billing | Viewer |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| See balance, usage, request history, results, transactions, invoices, and members | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Run APIs from the dashboard (Playground, company research) | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Add funds and change auto top-up | Yes | No | Yes | No |
| See API keys | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Create, edit, rotate, disable, and delete API keys | Yes, any access level | **Run APIs** and **View account** keys only | No | No |
| Connect apps such as the CLI or an MCP client | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Disconnect apps | Any app | Apps they connected | No | No |
| Change the response retention setting | Yes | No | No | No |
| Invite people, change roles, and remove members | Yes | No | No | No |
| Rename or delete the Team | Yes | No | No | No |

A Developer can't create, edit, rotate, re-enable, disable, or delete a **Full access** key.
Rotating a key shows its new secret, so this keeps Full access in the hands of Admins.

Teams can't have **View seller earnings** keys. See [Personal-only features](#personal-only-features).

A request your role doesn't allow fails with **403** and error code `forbidden`.

Role changes take effect on your next request. An Admin changes a role from the **Members**
tab. A Team always needs at least one Admin, so the last Admin can't be given another role.

## Connected apps

When you approve an app such as the CLI (`anyapi login`) or an MCP client, it connects to the
account selected in the dashboard at that moment. Switch to the Team first to connect an app
to the Team. If you switch accounts while the approval page is open, the approval is refused;
reload the page and approve again.

On a Team, each connection belongs to the person who approved it:

- Only Admins and Developers can connect apps.
- Your connection is your own. A colleague connecting the same app doesn't disconnect yours.
- The connection follows your current role on every request. If you become Billing or
  Viewer, it can only read the balance and can no longer run APIs.
- If you're removed from the Team, your connections stop working at once.

## Remove a member or leave

An Admin removes a member from the **Members** tab. Removal takes effect at once:

- The person loses access to the Team on their next request.
- Apps they connected to the Team are disconnected.
- **Team API keys keep working**, including the keys that person created. Keys belong to the
  Team. Rotate any key the person could see. See [Rotate a key](/api-keys#rotate-a-key).

To leave a Team, ask an Admin to remove you. The last Admin can't be removed; make another
member an Admin first, or delete the Team.

## Rename a Team

An Admin renames the Team from the **Settings** tab of the **Members** page.

## Delete a Team

An Admin deletes the Team from the **Settings** tab. Deleting removes the Team for everyone,
and its API keys and connected apps stop working. It can't be undone.

AnyAPI refuses to delete a Team while money could still reach it or work is still running:

| The Team | Error code | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Still has a balance | `team_has_balance` | Spend the balance down to $0, then delete. |
| Has auto top-up turned on | `team_auto_topup_on` | Turn off auto top-up, then delete. |
| Has a payment that is still completing | `team_payment_pending` | Try again once the payment has landed. |
| Has requests still running | `team_busy` | Try again when they finish. |

When a deletion is refused, the Team stays exactly as it was.

### A Team that is closing

While AnyAPI deletes a Team, the Team is closing. If the deletion is interrupted before it
finishes, the Team stays closing until an Admin acts. While a Team is closing:

- Every API key, connected app, and member session is refused with **409** and error code
  `team_closing`.
- An Admin can choose **Retry deletion** or **Cancel deletion** on the **Settings** tab.

Cancelling returns the Team to normal, with its balance, keys, and members unchanged.

## Audit log

Admins can see who changed what on the Team, and when, in the [audit log](/audit-log).

## Notifications

Top-up receipts, low balance and balance-ran-out emails, and paused-key emails for a Team go
to every current Admin, at the email address of their own account.

## Personal-only features

These stay on your personal account and aren't available on a Team, for any role or key:

- **Sell APIs**: seller earnings, payouts, and the payout account. Teams can't have
  **View seller earnings** keys.
- **Referrals** and referral earnings.
- **Agent** and its Slack connection.
- The onboarding bonus and the free balance a new personal account starts with. A new Team
  starts at $0.
- Continuing an [agent's free trial](/agent-self-signup#upgrade-past-the-trial). Approve the
  upgrade from your personal account.

A Team API key or connected app that calls one of these gets **403** with error code
`forbidden`.
