---
title: "Audit log"
description: "See who changed what on your Team, and when."
---

The audit log is a record of changes made to a Team: who made each change, what changed,
and when. Use it to answer questions such as who created a key, who changed a spending limit,
or who removed a member.

The audit log is available on Teams only. Personal accounts don't have one.

## Open the audit log

Only a Team's Admins can see the audit log. Switch to the Team, then open **Account** >
**Audit log** in the dashboard sidebar, below **Members**.

Entries are grouped by day. Each entry shows:

- The time of the change.
- Who made it, and the role they had at that moment.
- What changed, such as a key's old and new name.

Names stay readable after a person leaves the Team or a key is deleted.

## What the audit log records

| Area | Changes recorded |
| --- | --- |
| API keys | Created (with its name, access level, expiry, and spending limit), renamed, spending limit changed, disabled, enabled, rotated, and deleted |
| Team | Created, renamed, billing email changed, deletion started, deletion cancelled, and a deletion that didn't finish and left the Team active |
| Members | Invited, invitation resent, invitation cancelled, joined, role changed, and removed (with how many connected apps were disconnected) |
| Connected apps | An app such as the CLI or an MCP client approved or disconnected |
| Settings | How long API responses are stored, and auto top-up |

API key values never appear in the audit log.

Auto top-up changes are recorded once the payment service confirms them, so in rare cases one
can be missing from the log.

## What it doesn't record

- **API requests.** See your request history.
- **Money in and out.** See **Wallet activity** on the **Billing** page.
- **Sign-ins.**

The audit log starts on the day the feature launched. Changes made before then aren't in it.

## How long entries are kept

Entries are kept for as long as the Team exists.

## Download the log

Choose **Download CSV** to export the whole audit log. The file has these columns:

| Column | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| time | When the change happened, in UTC |
| who | The name of whoever made the change |
| who type | Whether a person, an API key, a connected app, or AnyAPI made the change |
| role | Their role at the time of the change |
| what | The change, in words |
| action code | A code for the kind of change, such as `key.created` |
| target | The key, member, invitation, app, or Team that changed |
